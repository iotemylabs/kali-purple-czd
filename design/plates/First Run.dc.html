THE CALL · Batch 03 drew the boot chain as four still plates and said nothing about time. This batch covers the moments between them and after them: what the splash does while it waits, what the login does when you get it wrong, what the machine says on the first boot, and how it interrupts you afterwards. All four are places a respin usually leaks its upstream — a stock Plymouth spinner, a Breeze notification, an Adwaita toast.
SETTLED IN 05, DRAWN HERE · The panel is at the bottom — Kali's default, visible on both desktop plates below — and CZD's defensive tooling opens the menu at 14, after Kali's 01–13, which is the line the welcome window leads with. Nothing in Kali's numbering moved, so a hand that knows where 06 Sniffing is still finds it there.

01 · A TIMING SHEET, NOT KEYFRAMES
There is no animation to storyboard, so the spec is a table of values and the moments they change. Every block is a state that holds until something real happens — a udev event, a mount, a progress callback. Scrub the boot progress tweak to put the playhead anywhere and read the frame off the lanes.
02 · SEVEN STEPS, NOT SIXTY FPS
Plymouth will happily run a redraw loop. Ours returns early unless the progress value actually moved, so the track advances in the seven jumps the system reports and the GPU does nothing in between. On a 4s SSD boot you see three of them.
03 · THE HANDOFF IS THE HARD PART
Most respins flash a black frame between splash and login. --retain-splash holds the last frame on the framebuffer while SDDM starts on the same plate, so the transition is the type changing and nothing else. Verify it on the slowest laptop in the build fleet, not the fastest.
04 · ESC STILL WORKS
Pressing ESC drops the splash and shows the systemd log, as it does on stock Kali. The theme does not trap the key, does not re-enter, and does not hide failures behind the mark — if the boot stalls, the person in front of it should be able to see why.

01 · NOTHING MOVES ON A MISS
No shake, no flash, no red. The two fields stay exactly where they were and a block appears below them — the layout grows down into space that was already empty. A person retyping a password should not have to re-find the field.
02 · GOLD IS THE ERROR COLOUR
There is no red in this system and a failed login is not an emergency. The block takes the same 0.05 tint and 2px caps as an emphasis row on a schedule board. Status colour is reserved for HOLD and STALE, and neither applies to a typo.
03 · SAY THE RULE UP FRONT
The copy names the attempt, the caps-lock state and the lockout before you hit it — same stance as the LUKS prompt in batch 03. "No dice" is the whole apology. Nothing tells you to contact your administrator; on a live USB you are the administrator.
04 · SESSION AND POWER
Both corners are gold mono labels with an arrow attached to the label, not icons and not buttons. Their popovers open upward on the inset surface with 1px hairlines between rows — the wayfinding pattern, reused. Wayland is the default; X11 is in the list for the one driver that needs it.

01 · IT IS A LIST, NOT A TOUR
No carousel, no five-step wizard, no "get started" hero. The useful content is four rows of the same time-plus-content pattern the schedule boards use, with the label column carrying a word instead of a clock. You can read the whole thing standing up in about fifteen seconds.
02 · THE CHECKBOX IS OFF
Default is do not open again, opt in to keep it. A welcome window that reappears every boot is a bug with a checkbox. Autostart removes its own .desktop file on first close; czd-welcome from a terminal brings it back.
03 · ONE FILLED GOLD THING
CLOSE is the only filled-gold element on the screen, which makes it the loudest and puts the exit where the eye already is. THE MENU → is a hairline box beside it. Gold-on-#0A0A0A inverted, no radius, no hover lift — the tint arrives, that is all.
04 · THE PANEL, FOR REAL
First plate to show the settled call: 56px at the bottom, launcher reading CZD ▸, task entries marked by a 1px gold underline rather than a filled pill, and five 32px tray cuts from batch 05. Nothing floats, nothing is translucent, nothing auto-hides.

01 · A NOTIFICATION IS A ROW
Source and time on one mono line, an Archivo headline, a mono detail line, gold mono actions. Same anatomy as a schedule row rotated upright, and the 2px gold cap on top is the same rule that opens a QR block. No app icon, no avatar, no colour coding by severity.
02 · THEY DO NOT SLIDE IN
Plasma's default is a slide-and-fade; CZD sets the popup animation duration to 0ms. A notification is present on one frame and absent on the next. Ageing past 30s drops the block to the 45% past-row opacity — see the third one — then it goes.
03 · THE OSD IS A LABEL AND A RULE
Volume, brightness, caps lock and mic mute all use one 480×96 block: a gold mono word, a tabular value, and the 2px track. No dial, no speaker glyph, no big centred number. Mute shows the word MUTED and an empty track — the state is written, not drawn.
04 · DND IS THE TALK-TRACK DEFAULT
Suricata on a conference network will shout. The build ships a PRESENTING shortcut that flips DND and holds everything; the tray cut takes the 45° strike and the stack collapses to one honest line with a count. Flip do not disturb in Tweaks to see it.
