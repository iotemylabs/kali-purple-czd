THE CALL · Batches 01–06 covered surfaces CZD owns outright. This one covers the surfaces it only borrows: the terminal, and every application that arrives wearing someone else's toolkit. Wireshark is GTK, Burp is Java, Arkime is a browser, and none of them read a Plasma colour scheme. Left alone they land in Adwaita grey and undo six batches of work in one window.
THE HARD PART · A terminal needs sixteen distinguishable colours and CZD has one hue. `ls` has to separate a directory from an archive, `git diff` has to separate an addition from a deletion, and nmap has to separate open from closed — in gold alone all three become guesswork. So 07 introduces exactly one sanctioned exception, scoped to the inside of a terminal box, and specifies it tightly enough that it cannot leak onto a signage board.
DRAWN AT 24PX · Terminal and packet type ships at 11pt / 14.7px on a 1080p screen. These plates draw it at 24px so it survives review at arm's length; the ratios, colours and rules are the shipping values.

01 · THE PROMPT IS A RULE, NOT A THEME
Two lines: a gold context line — user, host, path, interface or git branch — and a bare $ you type on. No powerline segments, no arrow chevrons, no glyph font, no exit-code emoji. Command history stays readable because the context line is the only coloured thing in it.
02 · OPAQUE, ALWAYS
Konsole's default profile is 90% translucent with blur. CZD sets opacity 1.0 and no background image: a terminal on a projector has to be readable from the back of Geary Auditorium, and a wallpaper showing through packet output is a demo hazard.
03 · BLOCK CURSOR, NO BLINK
A 13×30 gold block, cursor colour #E0A82E, blink off — the system's longest sanctioned motion is 90ms and a blinking cursor is an infinite animation. Bell is visual and it is the 0.05 gold tint for one frame, never a beep in a full lecture hall.
04 · TABS ARE UNDERLINED, NOT FILLED
Same treatment as a panel task entry from 04: 0.05 tint plus a 1px gold bottom border. Tabs are numbered because Alt+2 is faster than reading, and a closed tab leaves no ghost — the strip shows what exists, nothing more.

WHERE IT MAY NOT GO
Signage, print, web, Plasma chrome. Tools emit ANSI we do not control; CZD's own type never does.
01 · ONE HUE IS NOT ENOUGH HERE
Every other CZD surface can be gold-only because CZD writes the content. A terminal renders output from someone else's program — nmap decides open is green, git decides deletions are red. Refusing colour would not make the terminal purer, it would make it lie about what the tool said.
02 · DERIVED, NOT PICKED
Each hue is the gold ramp rotated in OKLCH and held at one of two luminance bands — L≈0.58 normal, L≈0.70 bright — with chroma capped at 0.06. That is why the ramp reads as one family in low light and why no slot fights the gold: they are the same lightness as the ink around them.
03 · EVERY SLOT CLEARS 4.5:1
Most terminal schemes ship a normal-intensity red or blue at 3:1 and rely on you leaning in. All fourteen foreground slots here clear 4.5:1 on #050505, worst case red at 4.8. Slot 00 is background only and is the one entry with no ratio.
04 · YELLOW IS THE BRAND, LITERALLY
Slot 03 is #E0A82E and slot 11 is #FFE9A8 — the brand gold and the surge, unmodified. So a `grep --color` hit and a selected schedule row on the lobby board are the same colour, and the terminal is inside the system rather than beside it.

RULES OFF ·
Wireshark's stock rules paint whole rows in saturated pastels and override any GTK theme. With them off the list is ink-only and protocol becomes a word you read rather than a colour you guess — legible, and slower.
ALTERNATING ROWS #050505 / #0A0A0A · SELECTION IS 0.05 TINT + 2 HAIRLINES, NEVER A FILLED BAND
01 · GTK GETS A REAL STYLESHEET
Not a recolour of Adwaita. czd-purple-gtk is a GTK3 theme built from the same tokens: #0A0A0A window, #050505 inset, gold hairlines, radius 0, and the header bar dropped to a plain 48px title strip so a GTK app and a Qt app read as the same window.
02 · THE COLOURING RULES ARE THE THEME
Wireshark paints rows from its own `colorfilters` file, which no theme can reach. So the respin ships one: protocol in the instrument palette on the row's own surface, foreground only. Retransmissions and alerts get bright red text, not a red row.
03 · SELECTION IS THE SCHEDULE ROW
The selected packet takes the 0.05 gold tint and 1px gold rules top and bottom — the emphasis row from a lobby board, at 20px, in a packet list. One filled-gold element per screen is the rule, and in this window it is APPLY's focus ring, not the row.
04 · WHY WIRESHARK FIRST
It is the worst case: GTK, its own colour engine, four panes, a monospace hex dump, and the app most likely to be on the projector. If the toolkit pass survives this window it survives Dolphin, GIMP and the file picker — the three places a theme usually breaks quietly.

01 · SAY WHAT YOU CANNOT REACH
Most respins claim a themed desktop and quietly leave Burp in Metal grey. The PARTIAL and NONE chips use the only two non-gold hues the system has — the same ones a signage board uses to admit it has nothing live. Marking the gap is the same move as a gold TBC.
02 · GENERATED, NOT MAINTAINED
Five toolkits means five syntaxes for the same sixteen colours. A build script reads the token JSON and writes all six — including the vim/neovim colorscheme, the one editor CZD ships the slots to — so changing the gold is one edit and a rebuild — not a hunt through .kvconfig, gtk.css and .Xresources for hexes that drifted apart.
03 · VENDOR UIS KEEP THEIR BRAND
Arkime and Velociraptor are other people's products with their own identity, and a conference respin repainting them is both fragile and rude. CZD themes the window around them and stops. That boundary is also the honest answer to "is this a distro or a paint job" — it is a paint job, deliberately.
04 · SCROLLBARS ARE THE TELL
The fastest way to spot an unthemed app is the scrollbar: 12px, arrows, a rounded thumb. CZD's is 8px, no arrows, square, 26% gold thumb on the inset surface, and it is specified once per toolkit. Fix the scrollbar and half the leak is gone.
BATCH COMPLETE · The terminal, the sixteen-slot instrument palette that makes it honest, the GTK pass on the hardest window in the build, and a coverage table that names the four toolkits CZD cannot fully reach. With 07 the respin is consistent from firmware to the inside of a packet list.
SETTLED · The instrument palette is approved as drawn, scoped to the terminal and the packet list and nowhere else — six hues that exist in CZD only where a tool, not CZD, chose the colour. The prompt ships two-line. Beyond the five toolkit files, the only editor to consume the sixteen slots is vim / neovim; tmux, bat, delta and htop stay on their own defaults. Build string across every surface is CZD-2026-10-231.
