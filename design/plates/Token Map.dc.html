WHAT I DID
state.success
CZD defines one accent hue and two greys. A package manager that cannot say "done" in green is a worse OS.
New hue #4FC26B — green pulled warm and desaturated toward the gold field. 8.73:1.
state.error
Same reason. SDDM "Login failed" and ANSI red need a hue the eye reads as failure in under a second.
New hue #E5484D — red warm-shifted off pure. 5.06:1.
font.ui
CZD reserves Archivo for headlines and sets everything else in mono. A whole desktop UI in mono is hostile at 10px.
No new font. Archivo's role widened to UI body; mono keeps every structural label, so the terminal character survives.
surface.overlay
CZD has four surfaces but no named overlay plane; Plasma menus need one distinct from the panel.
No new value. #181307 (CZD "warm well") reassigned to the overlay role.
Also flagged, not a colour: your sponsor list has four tiers (Title / Elite Partner / Supporting / Community Advocate), not the briefed three. wallpaper-sponsors will be laid out as 1 / 4 / 4 / 4 slots with tier-collapse rules.
