01 · THE ROW, AGAIN
GRUB entries are the CZD time-plus-content row with the time column carrying a 260px gold token instead of a clock. Rows touch; 1px 13% hairlines between, 2px 26% at the stack's foot. Same rhythm as a schedule board, so the boot menu and the lobby signage are legibly one system.
02 · SELECTION
Selected row takes the 0.05 gold tint plus 2px gold rules top and bottom, and its token goes to surge #FFE9A8 — the one surge on the screen. GRUB paints selection from 9-slice pixmaps, so this ships as select_*.png (3px corners, 2px edges), not as a colour property.
03 · FONTS ARE A BUILD STEP
GRUB reads only .pf2. Build runs grub-mkfont at the four sizes used here — Archivo 600 at 40, Plex Mono 700 at 30 and 24, Plex Mono 400 at 26 — into czd-*.pf2. No hinting below 24px because GRUB has no subpixel AA and 1080p menu text is read from a chair, not a lobby.
04 · TIMEOUT
A progress_bar with id __timeout: 2px, full content width, gold on #4A320B, no bar frame, no text inside. Default 8s (set it in Tweaks). It shortens left to right and vanishes on any keypress — GRUB's only motion, and it is a value change, not an animation.

01 · FOUR SPRITES
Plymouth's script module composites images, so the splash is four sprites on one baked background: mark, wordmark, 2px track, and the text line. Everything is centred on x960; the stack occupies y380→700 and never enters the bottom 112px where the systemd log takes over on ESC.
02 · PROGRESS, NOT SPINNER
No spinner, no throbber, no pulse — CZD has no rotating artwork. The 640×2 track fills gold from the left on real boot_progress callbacks, redrawn in steps, never eased. Drag it in Tweaks to check any frame. If plymouthd reports no progress, the track stays empty rather than faking motion.
03 · BAKED BACKGROUND
The field is a flattened PNG of the dim lattice under the heavy vignette — identical file family as wallpaper-lock, so the handoff from splash to login is one continuous plane. Plymouth cannot render gradients or SVG at runtime; the 1920×1080 plate ships in the theme dir and scales by Window.GetWidth() ratio.
04 · TYPE IN THE INITRD
Image.Text needs the fonts inside the initramfs. Hook copies Archivo and Plex Mono into /usr/share/fonts/czd/ and the theme declares them by family name; verify after every update-initramfs — a missing font silently falls back to DejaVu and the splash stops being CZD.

01 · ONE DELTA
Same theme, same background, same mark. The progress track is replaced by the field block and the stack lifts 80px (top y380 → y300) so the added 174px still clears the log zone. Nothing else moves — the two states read as one screen gaining a field, not two screens.
02 · THE WELL
640 × 72 on #050505 surface.inset with a 1px gold border, and because it is always focused it always carries the 1px gold ring at 2px offset. Bullets are the literal • glyph at 0.30em, count-accurate to keystrokes. Caret is a 2×34 gold block that does not blink.
03 · HONEST FAILURE
Attempt counter is stated up front, not after the first miss. On a wrong passphrase the label goes to state.error, the counter ticks, and the copy says what happened — "no dice, 2 of 10" — no shake, no flash, no red field. After ten the seat holds for 60 seconds and offers ten more. Caps-lock state is shown before it bites.
04 · WHO SEES IT
Installed systems with full-disk encryption, and the PERSIST live entry. The plain live boot never shows this frame — if you toggle persistence off in Tweaks, the GRUB row and this surface leave the build together.
